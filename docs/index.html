Whether you're a 2.0 just off your first open play or chasing your next DUPR milestone, the Genie builds the practice plan — you bring the paddle.

What the Genie grants you

Workouts built for you

AI-generated sessions sized to the time you actually have: a warm-up, a sequenced drill block, and a cool-down.

Guided Session mode

A full-screen runner with countdowns walks you through every drill, one step at a time, and saves the session to your history automatically.

Drill library

Browse 10 skill categories — dinking, drops, serving, resets, and more — with skill-level filters and video demonstrations.

“For You” picks

Recommendations tuned to your singles and doubles DUPR ratings and the parts of your game you want to grow.

60-second Skill Check

No rating? A quick, jargon-free self-assessment places you on the skill scale so day one still fits your game.

Learn the game

Scoring, serving rules, the kitchen, common faults, and a full glossary — readable offline, no signal required.

Video self-review

Record your strokes and review them frame by frame against a form checklist. Your clips never leave your device.

Tournament finder

See upcoming events near your home city so your training has something to point at.

Streaks & weekly goals

Watch the work add up with training streaks and weekly targets — and take your drills to the court with offline caching.
