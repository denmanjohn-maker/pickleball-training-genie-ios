Pickleball Genie builds training sessions around your game. Tell it your DUPR ratings, how you like to play, and how long you have, and it generates a workout of real drills matched to your level — then keeps a history of what you've done so your training keeps moving forward.

What's inside

  • AI-generated workouts — a warm-up, drill block, and cool-down sized to the time you have.
  • Drill library — browse and filter drills by skill category and target level.
  • Personalized recommendations — drills picked for your singles and doubles ratings.
  • Tournament finder — upcoming events near your home city.
  • Workout history — every completed session, so you can see the work add up.

Availability

Pickleball Genie is an iPhone app for iOS 17 and later.
